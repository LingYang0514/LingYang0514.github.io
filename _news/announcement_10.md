Ling Yang attended the 2025 Materials Research Society (MRS) Fall Meeting & Exhibit, held from November 30 to December 5, 2025, in Boston, Massachusetts, at the Hynes Convention Center and the adjacent Sheraton Boston Hotel. The meeting showcased interdisciplinary research in fundamental and applied materials science and provided opportunities for technical exchange and collaboration across the global materials research community.

[Meeting information](https://www.mrs.org/meetings-events/annual-meetings/2025-mrs-fall-meeting)
