Ling Yang attended the 2025 Materials Research Society (MRS) Spring Meeting & Exhibit, held from April 7 to 11, 2025, in Seattle, Washington. The meeting brought together researchers from across materials science to exchange technical information, discuss interdisciplinary advances, and explore emerging directions in the field.
