Ling Yang was invited to share the Science paper "Selective directional liquid transport on shoot surfaces of Crassula muscosa" at iCANX First Author Live Vol. 02. The talk introduces the discovery of a succulent-inspired liquid transport mechanism and its potential for programmable microfluidics and reconfigurable liquid manipulation.
