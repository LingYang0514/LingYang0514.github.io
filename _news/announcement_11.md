Ling Yang gave an academic talk at the Shandong-Hong Kong New Energy Talent Academic Forum and Shandong-Hong Kong Energy Students Forum, held from August 20 to 22, 2025, at Shandong University's Qianfoshan Campus. Her talk, titled "Selective Directional Liquid Transport Inspired by Crassula muscosa," was presented during the sub-forum alongside speakers from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, The University of Hong Kong, and City University of Hong Kong.
